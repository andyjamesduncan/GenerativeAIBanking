Global Wealth Strategy – House View Q4 2025
Executive Summary
Global growth remains resilient but uneven. Inflation has moderated but remains above target in
major developed markets. Central banks signal higher-for-longer rate stance. Recession probability
over next 12 months estimated at 30–40%. Investment stance remains neutral on global equities,
cautious on long-duration fixed income, and constructive on short-duration credit.


Macroeconomic Outlook
United States: Growth slowing but not contracting. Consumer resilience supported by labour market
strength. Inflation trending down but sticky in services. Euro Area: Weak manufacturing activity.
Energy costs stabilised but political fragmentation risks persist. Emerging Markets: Mixed outlook.
Select Asian economies benefiting from supply chain reallocation. Central banks expected to
maintain restrictive policy through Q1 2026.


Asset Allocation Guidance
Strategic allocation bands: Equities: 50–65% Fixed Income: 25–40% Cash: 5–15% Tactical
positioning: Neutral global equities Underweight long-duration government bonds Overweight
short-duration investment-grade credit Neutral high-yield bonds Neutral emerging market equities


Equity Strategy
Prefer high-quality large-cap equities with strong balance sheets and pricing power. Favour
healthcare and technology sectors. Avoid highly leveraged cyclical businesses. Dividend
sustainability prioritised over yield maximisation.

Fixed Income Strategy
Duration positioning below benchmark. Prefer short-duration corporate credit. Avoid concentrated
exposure to lower-tier high-yield issuers. Maintain selective exposure to inflation-linked securities.


Risk Management Considerations
Monitor credit spread widening. Liquidity buffers maintained across portfolios. Stress testing
conducted for 150bps parallel shift in yield curve. Volatility expectations remain elevated into
upcoming central bank meetings.


Client Suitability Framework
Portfolio adjustments must remain within strategic allocation bands. Any allocation outside
approved bands requires CIO approval. Enhanced suitability documentation required for
high-volatility asset classes.
