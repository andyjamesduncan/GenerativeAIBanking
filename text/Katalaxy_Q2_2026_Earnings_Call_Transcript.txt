CEO – Andrew Hale:
Looking ahead, we expect revenue growth in the mid-single digits for the remainder of

Risks remain in three areas:
First, regulatory change in the EU could increase compliance costs further.
Second, customer procurement cycles are lengthening in the enterprise segment.
Third, foreign exchange volatility may aRect reported earnings.
