Monthly Market Summary –
(June 2024)
Global equity markets rose modestly during the month, supported by stronger-than-expected
corporate earnings in the technology and healthcare sectors. The S&P; 500 increased by 2.4%,
while the Euro Stoxx 50 gained 1.8%. Emerging markets underperformed, declining 0.6%, largely
due to currency volatility and weaker industrial output data in Asia. Bond markets were mixed. U.S.
10-year Treasury yields rose from 4.10% to 4.25% following hawkish comments from the Federal
Reserve indicating that interest rates may remain higher for longer. European sovereign yields
remained broadly stable. Corporate credit spreads tightened slightly in investment-grade markets
but widened in high-yield bonds. Inflation data in the U.S. showed a marginal decline to 3.2%
year-on-year, though core inflation remains elevated at 3.7%. Oil prices rose 4% during the month
due to supply constraints and geopolitical tensions in the Middle East. Within the banking sector,
earnings were resilient, supported by higher net interest income. However, loan growth slowed, and
provisions for credit losses increased modestly. Currency markets saw the U.S. dollar strengthen
against most major currencies, including the euro and Japanese yen. Overall investor sentiment
improved, but volatility remains elevated ahead of upcoming central bank policy meetings.

Key Data Points:
• S&P; 500: +2.4%
• Euro Stoxx 50: +1.8%
• Emerging Markets: -0.6%
• US 10Y Treasury Yield: 4.10% → 4.25%
• US Inflation: 3.2% (Core 3.7%)
• Oil Price: +4%
• USD Strengthened vs EUR and JPY
